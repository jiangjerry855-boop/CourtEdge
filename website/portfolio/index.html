CourtEdge.

Does earlier time on court help predict the next tennis match?

The result

Adding prior workload to rankings and Elo did not reliably improve held-out predictions. On 4,871 held-out matches, log loss went from 0.61351 to 0.61361 (lower is better). The 95% event-bootstrap interval for the difference was −0.00075 to +0.00097.

How it was tested

The November 2026 model fit on pre-2026 matches and evaluated on held-out 2026 matches. The comparison uses identical evaluation matches. Workload measures minutes in earlier rounds, not fatigue directly.
